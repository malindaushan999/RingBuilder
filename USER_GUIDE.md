# Ring Builder - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 How to Use

### 1. Select Ring Type
Choose from 4 ring types:
- Engagement Ring (💑)
- Wedding Ring (💒)
- Eternity Ring (♾️)
- Promise Ring (💝)

### 2. Choose Metal Color
**Two ways to select:**
- **Click** on preset colors (Yellow Gold, White Gold, Rose Gold, Platinum)
- **Drag** a color to the ring preview below
- Use **custom color picker** for unique colors

### 3. Pick Diamond Shape
**Two ways to select:**
- **Click** on any shape icon
- **Drag** a shape to the ring preview

Available shapes:
- Round (💍)
- Princess (💎)
- Oval (✨)
- Emerald (🔷)
- Cushion (💠)
- Pear (💧)

### 4. Configure Diamond Specifications

**Carat Weight:**
- Use number input (0.5 - 5.0 ct)
- Impacts price significantly

**Color Grade:**
- D-F: Colorless (premium)
- G-H: Near Colorless
- I: Faint color

**Clarity:**
- FL/IF: Flawless (highest quality)
- VVS1/VVS2: Very Very Slightly Included
- VS1/VS2: Very Slightly Included
- SI1/SI2: Slightly Included

**Cut Quality:**
- Excellent (best light performance)
- Very Good
- Good
- Fair

### 5. Select Setting Style
- Solitaire (💍) - Classic single stone
- Halo (✨) - Diamond border
- Three Stone (💎) - Past, present, future
- Pavé (🌟) - Small diamonds on band
- Side Stone (💠) - Accent diamonds
- Vintage (🏛️) - Classic ornate design

### 6. Review Diamond Details
Check the **Diamond Details Panel** for complete specifications:
- All your selections summarized
- Verify before adding to cart

### 7. Adjust Currency & Language
**Currency Selector:**
- Click USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, or CAD
- Price updates instantly

**Language Toggle:**
- Click 🌐 button to switch
- Toggles between English/Spanish

### 8. Save to Wishlist ❤️
**Save Your Design:**
- Click "Save to Wishlist" button
- Design stored in browser
- Access anytime

**View Wishlist:**
- Click "View Wishlist" button
- See all saved designs
- Remove unwanted items
- Count badge shows total saved

### 9. Share Your Design

**Print Quote (🖨️):**
- Opens print dialog
- Professional quote format
- Includes image and specs
- Perfect for jewelers

**Email Quote (📧):**
- Opens email client
- Pre-filled message
- Complete specifications
- Share with friends/family

### 10. Add to Cart 🛒
- Click "Add to Cart" button
- Success notification appears
- Shows complete ring details
- Ready for checkout

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Drag & Drop Features
- **Metal Colors:** Drag from color picker to ring preview
- **Diamond Shapes:** Drag shape icon to ring preview
- **Visual Feedback:** Elements fade when dragging
- **Drop Zone:** Preview highlights when ready to drop

### Price Optimization
Higher prices = higher quality/value:
- **Ring Type:** Eternity > Engagement > Wedding > Promise
- **Shape:** Emerald/Pear > Cushion/Princess > Oval/Round
- **Color:** D > E > F > G > H > I
- **Clarity:** FL > IF > VVS1 > VVS2 > VS1 > VS2 > SI1 > SI2
- **Cut:** Excellent > Very Good > Good > Fair
- **Setting:** Three Stone > Vintage > Pavé > Side Stone > Halo > Solitaire
- **Metal:** Platinum > White Gold > Rose Gold > Yellow Gold > Silver

### Wishlist Management
- **Persistent Storage:** Designs saved even after closing browser
- **Multiple Designs:** Save unlimited variations
- **Compare Options:** View all saved designs side-by-side
- **Track Favorites:** Star your top choices

### Real-time Preview
- Image updates based on:
  - Metal type selection
  - Diamond shape choice
  - Ring type selected
- 18 unique ring images
- High-quality photos from Unsplash

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Image Not Loading?
- Fallback image automatically displays
- Check internet connection
- Refresh page if needed

### Wishlist Not Saving?
- Ensure browser allows localStorage
- Check browser privacy settings
- Clear browser cache and retry

### Price Not Updating?
- Each selection triggers recalculation
- All fields affect final price
- Currency conversion automatic

### Drag & Drop Not Working?
- Click as alternative method
- Ensure dragging to preview area
- Check browser compatibility
